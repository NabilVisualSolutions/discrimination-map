Terms of Use

What this tool is, what it isn't, and what we ask of everyone who uses it.

Last updated: August 2026
This page explains the rules in plain language. It isn't a substitute for legal advice, and using the site means you agree to it.

What this is

Discrimination Map documents publicly-reported hate incidents and, separately, solidarity/counter-protest events. It's a pattern-visibility tool, not a court, not a database of verdicts, and not staffed for emergencies. If you or someone else is in immediate danger, contact local emergency services — not this site.

Using it responsibly

  • Do: report incidents and events honestly, to the best of your knowledge. Review reports carefully and fairly if you're a moderator. Cite sources where you have them.
  • Don't: submit a report you know to be false, or file one to harass, target, or intimidate a specific private individual.
  • Don't: use this map, or any data from it, to build a target list, dox anyone, or coordinate harassment — against anyone, on any side of a report.
  • Don't: try to identify who filed an anonymous report, or scrape/bulk-export the map's data beyond ordinary use of the public site.
  • Don't: attempt to bypass moderation, spam the report form, or interfere with the service (rate-limiting, security testing without permission, etc).

Violating these terms can mean a report gets removed, an account gets revoked, or — for anything unlawful — a referral to the relevant authorities.

Leads, not verdicts

Nothing on this map is a legal finding. Automated and user-submitted reports are unverified by default; presumption of innocence applies to everyone named or described in a report until an actual court says otherwise. Treat every mark as "someone reported this," not "this is proven."

No warranty

We provide this map as-is, without guaranteeing its accuracy, completeness, or availability. It documents what was reported or found publicly — it doesn't independently verify facts on the ground, and it isn't legal, medical, or crisis advice. For real emergencies, use your local emergency number (110 in Germany).

Liability

To the extent the law allows, we aren't liable for decisions made based on this map's content, or for content submitted by users we didn't write ourselves. We do our best to moderate responsibly, but this is a volunteer-supported documentation project, not a guaranteed accurate record.

Requesting removal

If a report about you is wrong, unfair, or should never have been public, email dxmap@nabilvs.com with the report ID (shown as "DE-###" in the detail view) and what's wrong with it. We review every request personally — this isn't automated.

Governing law

These terms are governed by German law, where the project and its primary incident coverage are based. If you're using the site from elsewhere, your own local laws may also apply to what you post or how you use the data — that's on you to know.
