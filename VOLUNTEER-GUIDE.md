# Volunteering with Discrimination Map — how it works

## 1. Apply

Go to **nabilvs.com/projects/discrimination-map**, scroll to "Get involved."

Already signed into nabilvs.com? Your name and email are filled in automatically — no retyping, and no confirmation email needed, since your account email is already trusted.

Not signed in? Fill in:
- Name, email
- How you'd like to help — Moderator, Translator, Coder, Organization partner, Symbol hunter, or Other
- A short message (optional)
- Pass the captcha, submit

## 2. Confirm your email (only if you applied signed out)

Check your inbox for an email from **dxmap@nabilvs.com**. Click the confirm link — this just proves the email is really yours. Skip this step if you applied while signed in.

## 3. Create a nabilvs.com account (or log in)

Use the **same email** you applied with — email/password, Google, or GitHub sign-in all work. This is your nabilvs.com account, not a separate one for the map. (Already had one and applied signed in? You're done — skip to step 4.)

## 4. Check your status

Dashboard → **Volunteering**. Shows:
- Whether your email is confirmed
- Application status: Pending review → In progress → Approved (or Not moving forward)

## 5. If approved as a moderator

You'll get a **separate** login for **map.nabilvs.com/admin** — that's the actual tool for reviewing reports (verify or dismiss). Different login from your nabilvs.com account, on purpose: it's a different system, kept simple and separate.

## If you just want to document a banned symbol right now

No application needed. Go to **map.nabilvs.com**, tap **Report**, category "Banned symbol / salute." Anonymous, no account.

## Questions

Reply to the confirmation email, or use the message field when you apply.
